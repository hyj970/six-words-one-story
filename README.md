A complete story in exactly six words:

> "She kept his letters. Burned mine."

A love triangle in a single beat: she treasured his words, and mine went up in smoke. The turn lands in the last two words, and everything else — who he was, why she chose, what I did next — is left for the reader.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the six-word story to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (defaults to `/output/entry.txt`). Deterministic: the same constant string every run.
